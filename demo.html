Procurement Harness v0

A tiny audit surface for agent-card purchase attempts. The key behavior is not automatic checkout; it is pre-purchase constraint checking plus a human approval checkpoint.

Case Candidate Policy Checks Decision Reason
allowed Cloudflare Registrar domain, $12 Allowed merchant, allowed category, within $20 budget Approval Required All checks pass, but irreversible purchases require human approval.
over-budget BrowserStack monthly team plan, $199 Allowed merchant, allowed category, exceeds $25 budget Rejected Candidate price is above the maximum budget.
prohibited-category Amazon digital gift card, $25 Allowed merchant, prohibited cash-equivalent category, within $50 budget Rejected Cash-equivalent purchase is explicitly prohibited.

Receipt Redaction Policy

Receipts may retain merchant, item, price, timestamp, last four digits, and authorization status. Full card numbers, CVV, billing address, session tokens, and private order links must be removed.
